# Roadmap

## Phase 1
Binance public market scanner, quantitative features, heuristic scoring, FastAPI, mobile dashboard, backtest baseline, risk helper, Telegram hook.

## Phase 2
Binance/Bybit WebSockets, OI/funding/liquidation providers, trade-level CVD, order-book imbalance and absorption.

## Phase 3
Multi-timeframe SMC/ICT structure, liquidity maps, stop-hunt and fakeout state machines.

## Phase 4
Whale provider adapters, liquidation clusters, exchange flows, alerts and paper-trading journal.

## Phase 5
1000+ trade research harness, walk-forward/OOS validation, regime segmentation, Monte Carlo and realistic fee/slippage/funding models.

## Phase 6
Champion/challenger strategy versioning, research experiments and rollback.

## Phase 7
Only after paper validation: execution adapter with hard risk limits and explicit user enablement.
